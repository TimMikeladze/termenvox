What is a theremin?

The theremin is the first instrument played without touching it. Two antennas sense where your hands are: the vertical one sets pitch, the horizontal loop sets volume. Two radio oscillators beat against each other and the difference between them is the note you hear — heterodyning.

Leon Theremin (Lev Termen) built it in Russia around 1920 and called it the termenvox — "Theremin's voice". Clara Rockmore turned it into a concert instrument. Here your webcam replaces the antennas: by default the right hand takes pitch and the left rides volume, swappable under Gesture.
